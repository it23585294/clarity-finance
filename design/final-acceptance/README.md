# History correction - isolated visual evidence

After-only captures from the production screens with isolated fictional repositories, 5 November 2026. These are not live records, human timed captures, before/after comparisons or TalkBack evidence. The ordering scenario deliberately has two LKR34,000 entries with reversed IDs and known server-creation times of 10:00 and 10:02 Colombo; its LKR68,000 total is independent of the earlier human-trial baseline/delta. The second scenario checks known, unavailable and pending times.

The 320dp captures assert the real device screen width and **system** font scale2.0. Settings were restored to physical1080x1920/density420/font1.0/night-no. At enlarged text, rows and the information body scroll; amounts and timestamps wrap rather than shrink/truncate. The information dialog's Close control is exercised. The gallery does not imply every item fits the initial viewport at 2x.
